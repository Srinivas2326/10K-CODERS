Andhra Pradesh
Andhra Pradesh is a state located in the southeastern coastal region of India. Here's a summary covering some key aspects: Geography and Demographics: Andhra Pradesh has a long coastline along the Bay of Bengal. The state is known for its diverse geography, including fertile plains, hills, and the Eastern Ghats. As of recent estimates (though exact current figures require up-to-date census data), it has a significant population, with Telugu being the primary language. Major cities include Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, and Tirupati. Amaravati is the planned capital city. History: The region has a rich history, with mentions in ancient texts. It was ruled by various dynasties, including the Satavahanas, Chalukyas, Kakatiyas, and Vijayanagara Empire. Parts of Andhra Pradesh were also under British rule before India's independence. The state of Andhra Pradesh was formed in 1956 through the linguistic reorganization of states. Later, in 2014, the state was bifurcated, resulting in the formation of the separate state of Telangana.
